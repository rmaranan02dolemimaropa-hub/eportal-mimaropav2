Who We Are

About DOLE MIMAROPA E-Portal

Part of DOLE's digital transformation, the E-Portal brings regional labor and employment services online, so clients across Mindoro, Marinduque, Romblon, and Palawan can transact without the trip to a field office.

Fast Services

Streamlined request forms and automated routing cut processing time for the region's most requested documents.

Secure Transactions

Every request is encrypted end-to-end and logged, so your records and personal data stay protected.

Online Access

Available anytime from any device, so island-based clients no longer need to travel between provinces.

Our Commitment

Mission, Vision & Quality Policy

The principles that guide every service DOLE MIMAROPA delivers, online and in the field.

Mission

To promote gainful employment, protect workers' welfare, and maintain industrial peace across the MIMAROPA region through responsive, accessible, and client-centered labor services.

Vision

A MIMAROPA workforce that is empowered, protected, and globally competitive, supported by a labor administration that is digitally enabled and trusted by every client it serves.

Quality Policy

We commit to delivering efficient, transparent, and continually improving labor and employment services that meet client expectations and statutory requirements.
